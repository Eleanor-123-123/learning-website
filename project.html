Five ideas, not one

Project Ideas

A useful project begins with a clear subject and a real audience. These ideas all use film as a starting point, but solve different problems.

Things I might build

  • Film Review Notebook
  • Movie Night Planner
  • Quote Explorer
  • Genre Mood Map
  • Women Directors Guide

Who each one is for

Film Review Notebook
For students who want to remember what they watched and practise writing short, thoughtful reviews.
Movie Night Planner
For small groups that spend longer choosing a film than watching one.
Quote Explorer
For film lovers who remember a line but want to rediscover its speaker, film and context.
Genre Mood Map
For viewers who choose films by atmosphere rather than traditional genre labels.
Women Directors Guide
For new film students looking for an accessible path into work by women directors.

How I will build one

  1. Choose one audience and define the single main task.
  2. Sketch the page structure before writing code.
  3. Write semantic HTML and real content.
  4. Add a responsive layout with one shared stylesheet.
  5. Add one interaction, test every link and check the phone layout.
